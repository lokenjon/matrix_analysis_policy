# Privacy Policy for Acupuncture Tools App

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the Acupuncture Tools app ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). The App is designed for acupuncturists who use Dr. Tan methodologies, Five Element acupuncture or standard TCM.

## 2. Information We Collect
The App collects the following types of information:

**Analytics Data**
The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data and crash reports. This includes:
* App performance data
* Error logs and crash reports
* General usage statistics

**Authentication Information**
For certain features requiring authentication (such as AI services), we collect:
* Email address (when you sign in using Google or Apple Sign-In)
* Device ID (a unique identifier for your device)
* User name (as provided by the authentication service)

For users signing in with Apple, you may choose to withhold your email address, in which case we will only receive your device ID.

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

**Analytics Data**
* Improving app performance
* Identifying and fixing errors
* Understanding general usage patterns to improve user experience

**Authentication Information**
* Providing access to protected features
* Preventing abuse of our AI services
* Enforcing usage limits
* Maintaining service quality

Your email address and device ID are used solely for service administration and abuse prevention purposes. We do not use this information for marketing or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

## 4. Data Storage
* Analytics data is stored by Google Firebase Analytics according to their data retention policies.
* Authentication information (email addresses and device IDs) is stored temporarily in server memory only. This information is not stored in any persistent database and is automatically cleared when our servers restart or after a period of inactivity.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not share your personal information with third parties except:
* Google (for Firebase Analytics)
* Authentication service providers (Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In) to verify your identity

The information collected is only accessible to the app developers for the purposes stated above.

## 6. Data Retention
* Crashlytics data (part of Firebase Analytics) is retained for 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted.
* Authentication information is only retained in server memory temporarily and is not persistently stored. This data is automatically cleared upon server restart.

## 7. User Rights and Contact Information
As your authentication information is only temporarily stored in memory and not persistently saved, there is no long-term record of your personal information on our servers. However, if you have any questions about this privacy policy or the app's data practices, please contact us via the email provided in the app.

## 8. Children's Privacy
The Acupuncture Tools app is not directed at or intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## 9. Security
While we only temporarily store authentication information in memory and do not maintain a persistent database of user information, we are committed to ensuring the security of our app and the data it processes through appropriate technical measures.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the "What's New" section in the app when releasing an update. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 11. Governing Law
This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Norway, as the app developer is based in Oslo, Norway.

## 12. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the email address provided in the app.
